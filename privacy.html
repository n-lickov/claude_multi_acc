Privacy Policy

Claude Mail is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own computer. It accesses Gmail (read and create drafts) and Google Drive (read-only) for the owner's own Google accounts.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server operated by the app, sold, or shared with third parties. Content is passed only to the AI assistant the owner uses, at the owner's request.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
